# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineers, technical peers, and prospective collaborators reading Mitch’s work and experience. Readers should be able to choose a case study or essay quickly, then inspect the argument and its evidence.

## Product Purpose

An engineering field journal and personal portfolio. Show what Mitch builds, his contribution, the decisions behind it, and the boundary between delivered results and experiments.

## Brand Personality

Precise, personal, inspectable. Preserve the established authorial voice and editorial identity.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS bento pages, fabricated metrics, decorative dashboards, inflated agency copy, repetitive ruled fact sheets, and animation that competes with reading.

## Design Principles

- Make the work easy to find and the evidence easy to inspect.
- Preserve factual and completion boundaries.
- Give each component only the width its content earns.
- Keep shared editorial rules consistent across pages.
- Treat tests and headed review as feedback for the design harness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA without claiming conformance from limited tests. Support both themes, keyboard focus, reduced motion, readable measures, and 44px standalone targets. Test phone, tablet, desktop, and ultrawide rendering.
