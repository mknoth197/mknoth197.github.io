## Scope

This chapter was about safely opening data up *inside* the company — making license data useful to internal teams without losing isolation or access-control guarantees, behind an application that carried a meaningful share of company revenue.

## What I built

- A **secure data-ingestion pipeline**, prototyped during a hackathon, that demonstrated license data could be shared internally while preserving data isolation and access controls.

## Outcome

The prototype made the business case for a dedicated investment — directly contributing to the formation of a new enterprise **Data Engineering team**.
