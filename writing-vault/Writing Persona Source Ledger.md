### Interaction-derived observations

- Prefers evidence-first engineering and durable receipts over plausible narration.
- Separates fact, inference, recommendation, authority, and completion status.
- Values privacy, caller scope, customer-visible behavior, and systems evidence over people scoring.
- Treats repeated failures as candidates for durable harness improvements.

## Source-use rule

For every attributed idea:

1. Link to the primary source.
2. Paraphrase the principle in original language.
3. State whether it supports a fact, inspires a lens, or provides an example.
4. Do not imply the source endorses Mitch's synthesis.
5. Do not reproduce distinctive wording beyond a short, necessary quotation.
