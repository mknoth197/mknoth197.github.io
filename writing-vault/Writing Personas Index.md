# Writing Personas

This vault is an editorial system for the personal website. It does not replace the author's judgment or manufacture a blended celebrity voice. Mitch is always the author. The other personas are lenses used to strengthen a draft.

## The personas

- [[Mitch Knoth Writing Persona]] — canonical voice and final authority
- [[Martin Fowler Editorial Lens]] — conceptual precision and evolutionary explanation
- [[Gene Kim Editorial Lens]] — systems, flow, feedback, and operational learning
- [[Jensen Huang Editorial Lens]] — first principles, strategic stakes, and full-stack ambition

## The workflow

- Start with [[Essay Brief Template]].
- Draft in the [[Mitch Knoth Writing Persona]].
- Run the draft through [[Editorial Council]].
- Verify attribution and boundaries against [[Writing Persona Source Ledger]].

## Governing rule

Use the distinguished-individual personas as bounded editorial lenses, never as impersonations. Do not write “as Martin Fowler,” “as Gene Kim,” or “as Jensen Huang.” Do not reuse distinctive phrases or verbal tics. Extract durable reasoning patterns, then express the result in Mitch's own voice.

## Default blend

The percentages are editing attention, not sentence-level mimicry:

- Mitch: 60% — thesis, evidence, field experience, and final wording
- Fowler lens: 18% — definitions, distinctions, and progressive examples
- Kim lens: 14% — system behavior, feedback, and learning
- Huang lens: 8% — stakes, first principles, and memorable compression

Change the blend by essay type, but never reduce Mitch below 50%.
